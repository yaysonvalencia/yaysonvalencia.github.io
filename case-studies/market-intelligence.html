The business decision

How should a consumer-electronics seller position products on Amazon India? The analysis examined price and discount patterns, customer ratings, review signals, and external competitive context.

The deliverable was a structured decision brief. It did not estimate sales, revenue, profitability, conversion, or market share from the product dataset.

My contribution

I defined the research objective and scope, configured a no-code workflow in Amazon QuickSuite, reviewed AI-assisted research and synthesis, evaluated confidence and source limitations, and prepared the final recommendations.

The workflow used an existing AI platform and a configured agent. The project demonstrates business workflow design and analytical review; it does not involve developing an independent AI model.

The analytical approach

  1. Frame the decision. Set the business question, included categories, exclusions, and non-goals.
  2. Combine evidence. Use the Kaggle marketplace dataset as internal knowledge and Quick Research for external market signals.
  3. Evaluate the synthesis. Separate dataset observations from external evidence; assess quality, consistency, timeliness, and confidence.
  4. Prepare recommendations. Tie each major insight to a business implication, limitation, and evidence that would change the conclusion.
The central control: popularity is not sales.

Rating count was treated as an engagement proxy. The analysis did not convert ratings or reviews into unit-sales or market-share estimates.

Findings and business relevance

The final brief develops five insights covering trusted accessories, budget-TV curation, premium positioning and financing, installation service, and pricing transparency.

What I’d do first

I’d audit a sample of Amazon India electronics listings for inaccurate specifications and misleading discounts. After correcting any factual errors, I’d test whether presenting verified specifications more clearly improves purchase conversion. The brief identifies credibility concerns, but the effect on purchases remains unmeasured.

Limits and next validation

  • The internal dataset is a static marketplace snapshot with uneven category coverage, no time series, and no verified sales.
  • External signals include single surveys, third-party estimates, and older market-share observations. The underlying external claims were not independently revalidated for this portfolio site.
  • Recommendations are hypotheses for testing. No measured revenue improvement, implementation outcome, or production deployment is claimed.

A business deployment would require current competitor evidence, verified sales and conversion data, and tests of the proposed positioning and service changes.
